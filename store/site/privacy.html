Droplio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Droplio (the "App") handles information. We keep this simple: the App does not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, unlocked skins and sound/music settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. If you delete the App, this data is deleted too.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to get an extra life or double coins). To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • IP address, used to estimate your approximate (coarse) location, such as your country or city
  • Information about the ads shown and your interactions with them
  • Basic device and app information, crash data and performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We do not receive any information that identifies you personally. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options.
  • On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google as described above, no data is shared with third parties.

6. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. Data collected by Google is protected according to Google's security practices.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: info@fabo-soft.com
